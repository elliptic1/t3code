# Mobile notifications

Sign in to T3 Connect, link your environments, and enable **Device Notifications** in **Settings → Notifications** to receive alerts when an agent finishes, fails, needs approval, or asks for input. Tap a notification to open its thread. Your environment must have agent activity publishing enabled.

Enable **Live Activity Updates** on iOS, **Agent Live Updates** on Android 16 and newer, or **Ongoing Agent Activity** on older Android versions to follow work without opening the app. Finished results remain visible for up to 15 minutes. You can dismiss an Android activity card without disabling alerts; turn off the same switch to stop future cards.

For activity without ordinary alerts on Android 8 or newer, leave **Ongoing agent activity** enabled and turn off **Agent alerts** in Android's notification settings for T3 Code. Activity updates continue, and the same card shows the finished result without a separate alert. On Android 16 and newer, the finished result remains a Live Update until dismissed or expired. Keep app-wide notification permission and **Live updates** enabled so Android can display it.

On iOS you can also add the **Agent Activity** widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen. It shows the same agents as the Live Activity, using your T3 Connect sign-in. The widget refreshes when you open T3 and shows unfinished work as out of date after 10 minutes.

Ordinary alerts stay quiet while the mobile app is in the foreground. Ongoing activity continues to update. Viewing a thread on another device does not silence your phone's alerts.

Android notifications require Android 7.0 or newer and Google Play services. Android 16 and newer can promote ongoing activity to a Live Update, subject to system settings and device support. Other devices show a regular ongoing notification. If Live Updates don't appear on Android 16 or newer, check **Live Update Settings** in T3 Code's **Settings → Notifications**. Android 7's battery-saving modes can delay removal of expired cards.
On Android, **Running thread usage** is a separate, quiet ongoing notification while threads are active. Expand it to see each running provider's allowance remaining, recent consumption in percentage points per hour, and an estimated exhaustion time. Estimates use up to 15 minutes of observed usage, need at least a minute of samples, and pause when readings are stale or the connection drops. Quota resets restart measurement. This monitor uses your existing paired connections, including direct and Tailscale connections, and keeps them alive until work finishes; it does not require T3 Connect. Allow notifications when prompted, or control its channel in Android Settings.

On Android, connected threads also show a **Thread input requests** notification when they need an answer or approval, including while the app is open. Expand it to reply to a single question, select a choice, approve once, or decline. Questions with multiple parts or multiple selections open in the thread instead. Tapping the notification opens the correct thread. Responses use your existing paired connection and are checked against the current request; if sending fails, the notification stays available for retry. Control this channel separately in Android Settings.

Android push notifications require Android 7.0 or newer and Google Play services. Android 16 and newer can promote ongoing agent activity to a Live Update, subject to system settings and device support. Other devices show a regular ongoing notification. Android 7's battery-saving modes can delay removal of expired cards.

Notification permission and Android notification channels are controlled in system Settings. Background delivery of agent alerts requires T3 Connect; a direct or Tailscale connection alone does not enable push notifications. Agent alerts do not require the mobile app to maintain a connection to your environment. Force-stopping the Android app in system Settings prevents background delivery and usage monitoring until you open it again.
